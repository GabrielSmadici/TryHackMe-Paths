These are my notes regarding phishing attacks and my undertanding of them.

Phishing uses social engineering to trick people into revealing sensitive information or running malware on their devices.

There are 3 types of phishing:

Phishing -> casting a wide net and cathing multiple people quickly with generic information.
Spear Phishing -> targeting a specific individual with the aim to move deeper into a organisation's network.
Whaling -> Spear Phishing but for seniour decision makers or executives (people with a high level of influence).

Common tools organisations use to combat against phishing:

DKIM - DomainKeys Identified Mail
DMARC - Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance
SPF - Sender Policy Framework

Tools for creating phishing campaigns in pentesting:

GoPhish -> web-based framework for setting up phishing campaigns, mainly based around sending phishing emails.
EvilNginx -> tool for advanced phishing that bypasses MFA acting as a reverse proxy.
SET (The Social Engineering Toolkit) -> a set of tools, with important ones mainly for spear phishing.
